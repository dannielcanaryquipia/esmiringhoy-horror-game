## 0. Your role and the rules of engagement

You are the lead gameplay programmer on **Esmiringhoy**, a first-person supernatural horror game made in **Godot 4 with GDScript**. A small working sample already exists: one bedroom, one demon, two candles. Your job is to turn it into a **suspenseful 20–35 minute horror experience**. The player explores a whole haunted Filipino ancestral house (*bahay na bato*), hides from the demon, solves tasks to perform a banishment ritual, and escapes before dawn.

Rules for how you work:

1. **Read before you write.** Read every file in `scripts/`, `scenes/`, `assets/shaders/`, `tools/` and `project.godot` before changing anything.
2. **Work in phases** (Section 13). At the end of each phase, stop and give me:
   - a summary of what changed (files added/changed),
   - how to test it (a manual playtest checklist, 5–10 steps),
   - the automated test results,
   - anything you were unsure about.
   **Do not start the next phase until I say "continue".**
3. **Check the Godot version first:** run `godot --version` (or ask me for the path to my Godot executable on Windows). Only use APIs that exist in that version. No Godot 3 APIs (`yield`, `KinematicBody`, `instance()`, etc.).
4. **Zero warnings policy.** The project must open and run with **no errors and no warnings** in the Godot Output/Debugger panel. Past warnings to avoid: unused parameters (prefix with `_`), and local variables or parameters that shadow built-in names (`show`, `hide`, `name`, `position`, `visible`, `scale`, etc.).
5. **Typed GDScript everywhere.** When a value comes from an untyped variable, declare the type explicitly (`var d: Vector3 = player.global_position - global_position`). `:=` on a Variant fails to parse in Godot 4.
6. **No downloaded assets, no third-party plugins, no addons.** Every texture, sound and model is generated by our own Python scripts in `tools/` or built from Godot primitives, CSG or script-generated meshes. All assets must be original.
7. **Don't break what works.** The flashlight, battery, freeze-in-beam, stutter, candle safe zone, catch sequence, red ghost vision and HUD all work now. Keep them working, or improve them deliberately and say so.
8. **Keep files small and focused.** No script over ~400 lines. Split by responsibility.
9. **Content tone:** supernatural dread and suspense. Dark, scary, tense. **No sexual content of any kind.** Violence is implied and cinematic (grabs, drags, screams, cut to black), not gore. The demon is a charred, horned, ember-cracked creature, as in the existing model.
10. When something in this brief is ambiguous, **pick the option that makes the game scarier but fairer**, note your choice and keep going. Ask me only about things that are expensive to undo.

**Lessons already learned. Don't repeat these bugs:**
- **Doorways must be at least 1.4 m wide.** The demon's navigation agent radius is 0.4 m. A 1.2 m door stopped the navmesh from connecting the hallway to the bedroom, and the demon got stuck against the frame next to the player forever.

- `room.gd` is a `@tool` script. Anything that only makes sense at runtime (audio, nav bake) must be guarded with `if not Engine.is_editor_hint()`.

## 2. The vision: what the game should feel like

**One-line pitch:** *You are trapped in your family's ancestral house on the night the Esmiringhoy wakes. Light the five candles, say the prayer, and get out before dawn. Don't let it find you.*

**Design pillars.** Every feature must serve at least one:

1. **Hunted, not chased.** Most of the fear comes from *knowing it is near and not knowing where*. Chases are short, rare and terrifying.
2. **Every safe place is temporary.** Hiding works, but the demon learns. Overused hiding spots get checked. Holding your breath only lasts so long.
3. **Light is a weapon and a beacon.** The flashlight freezes the demon but drains fast and gives away your position. Darkness hides you but blinds you.
4. **The house warns you, if you listen.** The demon always telegraphs: the bulb dies, the chair rocks, the kulambo sways, footsteps creak, whispers come closer. A good player survives by reading the house.
5. **Fair deaths.** Every death is explainable: *it saw you hide*, *it heard you run*, *you used that spot three times*, *you breathed*. The death screen tells the player why.

**Pacing target** (handled by the AI Director in Section 5.5): build-up → peak → relief → build-up. Never more than ~90 seconds without a sign of the demon. After a near-miss, at least 30 seconds of breathing room.

**Session length:** 20–35 minutes for a first full run on Normal. Replays are different because item locations and some puzzle answers are randomized.

### 3.2 Loops the player can use (must all exist and be walkable for the demon)

1. **Front-room enfilade ↔ Gallery:** Oratorio–Sala–Caida–Ang Silid are joined door to door, and the Gallery runs parallel behind them. Any two adjacent doors make a loop.
2. **Vertical loop:** Grand stair (Caida ↔ Zaguan) + back stair (Kusina ↔ Bodega) + Cuadra/Zaguan–Bodega doors. A full figure-eight through both floors.
3. **Wet loop:** Kusina → Azotea → Baño → Gallery → Kusina (outdoors in the rain, loud, and slippery: −15 % speed on wet tiles).
4. **Shortcuts:** the Comedor trapdoor drops you into the Bodega (one-way, loud landing, 15 m noise). The secret passage behind the aparador runs Ang Silid → Baño (must be discovered first by moving the aparador: hold E for 3 s, loud scrape).
5. **Dead ends:** Oratorio (locked until late) and Children's Room (one door). Dangerous, but they hold key items.

### 3.3 Room sizes and build rules

- Upper-floor footprint about 28 m × 20 m, ceilings 3.4 m. Ground floor ceilings 3.0 m.
- **All doorways ≥ 1.4 m wide, ≥ 2.1 m tall.** Stairs ≥ 1.4 m wide, steps the navmesh can climb (set `agent_max_climb` accordingly, or use a ramp collider under visual steps). Corridors ≥ 1.6 m wide.
- Keep the existing **Ang Silid** layout (bed, aparador, altar, vanity, tumba-tumba, broken floor, capiz windows) and fit it into the north-east corner.
- Moonlit patches under every capiz window (the demon avoids them) via the existing layer-2 trick.
- Each room has 1–3 light sources at most (moon, bulb, candle). Darkness is the default.
- **Ventanillas:** the small sliding panels under the capiz windows. Use them as peep holes. Some hiding spots let you peek through slats or ventanillas.

## 4. Hiding system (core new feature, build first)

### 4.1 Hiding spots

Create a reusable **`HidingSpot`** component (scene + script) that can be attached to furniture. Each spot defines:
- `spot_type` (enum): `UNDER_BED`, `WARDROBE`, `CURTAIN`, `UNDER_TABLE`, `CABINET`, `TUB`, `JAR`, `CARRIAGE`, `CLOCK_CASE`
- `enter_marker`, `exit_marker` (Marker3D), `camera_pose` (Marker3D for the hidden view)
- `concealment` (0–1): how hard it is to notice you from outside
- `check_anchor` (Marker3D): where the demon stands and which animation it plays to check this spot
- `heat` (float): rises each time the player uses this spot; decays over time
- `enter_time` / `exit_time` (seconds), `noise_on_enter` / `noise_on_exit`

| Room | Spot | Type | Concealment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ang Silid | Under the four-poster bed | UNDER_BED | 0.8 | Low camera (0.25 m). You see the demon's feet and dragging claws. **The signature scare.** |
| Ang Silid | Inside the aparador | WARDROBE | 0.7 | Peek through the door gap; the doors creak on entry |
| Children's Room | Under a small bed | UNDER_BED | 0.6 | Tighter, crouch-crawl only; the demon checks it faster |
| Sala | Behind heavy curtains | CURTAIN | 0.4 | Weak: your feet are visible. Detected if the demon passes within 1.5 m |
| Sala | Inside the grandfather clock case | CLOCK_CASE | 0.9 | Only available after the clock puzzle opens it; one use per run |
| Comedor | Under the long table (tablecloth) | UNDER_TABLE | 0.6 | Two sides to exit from (a mini-loop) |
| Kusina | Inside the paminggalan (slatted food cabinet) | CABINET | 0.7 | Peek through the slats |
| Baño | In the tub behind the curtain | TUB | 0.6 | Only after the tub is drained |
| Bodega | Inside a big tapayan (clay jar) | JAR | 0.85 | Almost no view out, only sound. Very scary |
| Zaguan | Inside the old carriage | CARRIAGE | 0.75 | Look out through the small carriage window |
| Gallery, Oratorio, Azotea, Caida | none | none | none | No hiding in corridors, the final room or outdoors |

### 4.2 Entering, staying, leaving

- Press **E** on a spot to enter (crouch is automatic). The enter animation takes `enter_time` (1.0 s; 1.4 s to crawl under a bed). During it you are vulnerable.
- While hidden: the flashlight can be toggled but **a lit flashlight inside a hiding spot = instant discovery** if the demon is within 10 m and facing your general direction. Show a subtle warning icon.
- Mouse look is limited to the spot's view cone (for example 70° horizontal under the bed).
- Press **E** to leave (`exit_time` 1.0 s). Leaving while the demon is within 6 m makes noise.

### 4.3 How the demon finds hidden players

Every death while hiding must come from one of these rules. The death screen shows which one.

1. **Seen entering:** if the demon had line of sight to the player during the enter animation or up to **2.0 s** before it, it *knows*. It walks straight to that spot and pulls you out. Death cause: *"It saw you hide."*
2. **Heard you:** noise made inside the spot (gasping after holding your breath, flashlight click, entering or leaving while it was near) raises **suspicion** on that spot.
3. **Spot heat:** using the same spot repeatedly makes the demon more likely to check it. +1 heat per use; −1 heat per 120 s. Death cause: *"It remembered your hiding place."*
4. **Search after losing you:** if the demon was hunting you and lost sight within the last 10 s, it enters **SEARCH**. It checks 1–3 hiding spots in the area, chosen by weighted random:
   `check_chance = clamp(0.20 + 0.20 * heat + suspicion + (1 - concealment) * 0.3, 0, 0.95)`
   On Normal, spots the player is *not* in are also checked, so the player sees it searching (tension), and the order is random so it isn't predictable.
5. **Light inside a spot:** a lit flashlight inside a hiding spot. Death cause: *"It saw your light."*

### 4.4 Hold your breath

- When the demon comes within **4 m** of your hiding spot, a breath prompt appears: hold **Space** (or right mouse button) to hold your breath.
- A breath meter drains over **6 s** (Easy 8 s, Nightmare 5 s) and refills at 1 per second after release.
- Not holding your breath while it is within 4 m: audible breathing → suspicion +0.15 per second.
- Running out (or releasing while it is within 2 m): **gasp** (6 m noise) → suspicion +0.5.
- Holding your breath muffles the audio (low-pass filter on the master bus), the heartbeat gets louder and the screen edges darken. This should feel suffocating.
- Fairness: on Normal, the demon's linger time at a spot it decided *not* to search (2–5 s) is always shorter than a full breath.

### 4.5 The under-bed check (the scene to get perfect)

The demon investigates the bed. The player is underneath, at floor-level camera height.

1. Footsteps approach. The player sees only the **feet and claw tips** at the edge of the bed, glowing scorch marks appearing on the floor where the claws drag.
2. The feet stop. Silence: drop the music stems, keep only a low drone.
3. **Kneel and peek:** the demon kneels (1.2 s). Its head lowers **sideways** into view at the edge of the bed, upside-down-ish, too-long neck twisting. Its eyes ignite red.
4. **If not detected:** it stares for 1.5–2.5 s (breath meter draining…), makes a wet sniffing sound, then the head slowly withdraws and the feet walk away. *This must be one of the scariest moments even when the player survives.*
5. **If detected:** a claw shoots in and grabs the player's ankle. The camera is **yanked backwards** out from under the bed (1.2 s, nails scraping on wood), flips to face up, and the demon looms over. Roar. Then the existing burn-out → turned sequence, or the death screen.

Make similar (shorter) check animations for each spot type: wardrobe (rips the doors open), curtain (tears it aside), table (lifts the tablecloth with one claw and peers in), cabinet (presses its face against the slats, eye visible through the gap), tub (slowly pulls the curtain), jar (taps the jar three times, then lowers its face over the opening), carriage (face appears at the carriage window).

### 4.6 Anti-cheese

- After **60 s** in the same spot, the house itself turns on you: whispers say *"Nakikita kita…"* ("I see you…") and the demon gets a strong hint toward that room. You can't win by hiding forever.
- The demon can't kill you inside a lit candle's safe zone, so **no hiding spot may be inside a candle's 2 m safe zone.**

## 5. Esmiringhoy AI 2.0

Refactor `demon.gd` into smaller parts: **`DemonBrain`** (state machine), **`DemonSenses`** (sight, hearing, light), **`DemonBody`** (the existing `demon_body.gd` plus new animations) and **`DemonAbilities`**. A node-based or class-based state machine is fine; keep each state small.

### 5.1 States

| State | What it does |
|---|---|
| DORMANT | Start of the game, until the first candle is picked up or 120 s pass (tutorial grace period) |
| PATROL | Walks between weighted patrol points (prefers rooms near the player's recent areas, with randomness). Whispers. Slow. |
| INVESTIGATE | Goes to the position of a heard noise; looks around for 3–6 s |
| HUNT | Saw the player: moves to last known position at hunt speed |
| CHASE | Short-range pursuit (< 8 m with sight). Chase music. Opens or bashes doors. Max 20 s, then the Director may break it off |
| SEARCH | Lost the player recently: searches nearby hiding spots (Section 4.3) |
| CHECK_SPOT | Plays the check animation for a specific hiding spot; kills if the player is there and detected |
| AMBUSH | (Phase 2+) Crawls onto the ceiling of a dark corridor near the player's predicted path and waits. Drops when the player passes under (survivable: a freeze-beam during the 0.6 s drop telegraph stops it) |
| FROZEN / STUTTER | Keep the existing freeze-in-beam and stutter behaviour |
| RETREAT | Director-forced: walks away to a far room and stays out of the player's area for 30–60 s |
| KILL | Plays the kill sequence (normal catch, under-bed drag-out, wardrobe pull-out, and so on) |
| ENRAGED | Final ritual only (Section 6.3) |
| BANISHED | Keep the existing dissolve |

### 5.2 Senses

- **Sight:** 110° cone up to 6 m, 70° cone up to 12 m. A player in darkness with the flashlight off is only seen within **4 m** (2.5 m if crouched). A lit flashlight is visible from **20 m** if its beam or bulb is in view, even through doorways.
- **Hearing:** create an autoload **`NoiseBus`** with `emit_noise(position: Vector3, radius: float, loudness: float, source: StringName)`. The demon hears a noise if its distance < radius, halved for each closed door or wall in between (use a raycast count, max 2).

| Source | Radius |
|---|---|
| Crouch walk | 1 m |
| Walk | 3 m |
| Sprint | 9 m |
| Door opened slowly (hold E) | 3 m |
| Door opened normally | 8 m |
| Door slammed | 15 m |
| Dropped or thrown item | 12 m |
| Breathing (not holding breath near it) | 2 m |
| Gasp | 6 m |
| Falling through the broken floor or trapdoor | 15 m |
| Fuse box power-on | 30 m (whole house) |
| Tub draining | 18 m, for 20 s |
| Music box lure | 20 m, for 20 s |
| Piano note | 12 m |
| Creaky floorboard zones | 7 m |

- **Light sense:** it notices lit bulbs or candles changing state anywhere on its floor.

### 5.3 Memory

The demon remembers:
- the last known player position (+ time),
- hiding-spot heat (Section 4.3),
- which rooms the player visits most (rolling 3-minute window) and patrols there more often,
- loops: if the player loops the same pair of doors 3 times during a chase, it **turns around** and cuts the loop off from the other side.

### 5.4 Abilities by ritual phase (escalation)

| Candles lit | Demon changes |
|---|---|
| 0 | Patrol 1.8 m/s, hunt 4.0 m/s. Opens doors normally |
| 1–2 | Hunt 4.2 m/s. **Bashes** closed doors it can't open (2 s, 15 m noise; the door breaks permanently). Investigates noises faster |
| 3 | **Ceiling crawl** in dark corridors (AMBUSH state). Kills one random bulb per minute |
| 4 | **Mirror walk:** it can vanish into any mirror and step out of another mirror in the house (the vanity mirror, a Sala mirror, a Baño mirror). Telegraph: the target mirror's glass fogs and cracks 2 s before. Freeze-in-beam lasts 30 % shorter |
| Final rite | ENRAGED (Section 6.3) |

### 5.5 The AI Director (pacing)

Create an autoload **`Director`** that watches a **menace** value (0–100):
- Rises when the demon is near the player (closer = faster), has line of sight, is in CHASE, or kills a light near the player.
- Falls slowly when the demon is far.
- If menace stays **> 85 for 8 s** (or right after the player survives a CHECK_SPOT or a chase), force **RETREAT** for 30–60 s. Relief.
- If the player hasn't had any demon sign for **90 s**, nudge the demon toward the player's *general area* (a random point 8–15 m from the player). Never their exact position.
- The Director also drives the **AudioDirector** (Section 8.2) and schedules **environmental events** (Section 8.3) during low-menace periods, so scares happen when the player is calm, not stacked on top of a chase.

### 5.6 Fairness rules for the AI

- The demon **never spawns or teleports into the player's view**, and never inside a 6 m radius of the player.
- Before entering the player's room there is always at least one telegraph (footsteps, a growl, the bulb dying, the chair rocking, the kulambo swaying, a mirror fogging).
- A kill always follows a detectable mistake (Section 4.3), a lost chase, or standing in the open within its sight. No random instant deaths.

## 6. Objectives: the banishment ritual

### 6.1 Story (short, told through notes)

The family who owned this house made a bargain long ago. The **Esmiringhoy** was the price. It wakes once a generation, and tonight it is your turn. Your *lola*'s (grandmother's) notes explain the **Oración de Pagpapaalis** (the banishment prayer): five blessed candles, lit in the right order in the Oratorio, then the prayer said aloud. Then leave the house before dawn and never look back.

Write about 8 short notes (40–90 words each) found around the house: diary pages, a death certificate, a child's drawing, a prayer card, a letter. Keep them atmospheric and a bit cryptic. Some contain puzzle clues. English text with occasional Filipino words (*lola, apo, oración, santo, kandila, anting-anting*).

### 6.2 Task chain (one full run)

1. **Wake up in the Zaguan.** The front door is barred. A note says: *"Light the five candles. Say the prayer. Leave before dawn."* The flashlight is on the carriage step. Tutorial prompts here (move, flashlight, crouch, hide, hold breath). The demon is DORMANT.
2. **Find matches** (a box of 8 on Normal). Each lighting attempt uses one match. A match can also be struck as a 15 s emergency light (weak, warm, doesn't freeze the demon).
3. **Restore power:** find **3 fuses** (randomized among about 8 possible spots) and install them in the Bodega fuse box. Power-on is a **30 m noise**: the demon comes. Afterwards some bulbs work (safe-ish light, but they still die when the demon is near). Unlocks the **Kusina back stair light** and the **Sala clock mechanism**.
4. **Get the five candles.** Each one is behind a mini task. Build all six; each run uses five, chosen at random:

   | # | Room | Task | Risk / thrill |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | Sala | **Grandfather clock:** set the hands to the time of death on the death certificate (Ang Silid vanity drawer; time randomized from a pool) → the clock case opens: candle inside, and the case becomes a hiding spot | The clock **chimes loudly** when solved (12 m noise) |
   | 2 | Children's Room | **Music box:** find its missing crank (random: Kusina, Comedor or Bodega) → it plays → the toy chest unlocks with the candle. The music box can then be **carried and placed as a lure** (20 m noise for 20 s) | The melody also wakes "something" in the room: a child's giggle and footsteps upstairs (a hallucination event) |
   | 3 | Baño | **Drain the black water** in the tub (pull the chain) → candle at the bottom | Draining takes 20 s with an 18 m gurgle. You must survive or hide while it drains |
   | 4 | Ang Silid | **Vanity mirror:** a symbol only shows in the mirror when your flashlight is **off** and the moonlight is behind you → that symbol tells you which of the three altar drawers holds the candle | Wrong drawer = loud slam + the demon is alerted. You have to stand in the dark to read it |
   | 5 | Kusina | **Piano clue → paminggalan lock:** the Sala piano's sheet music has 4 marked notes; play them on the piano (noise 12 m per note) → a hidden compartment in the paminggalan opens | Playing the piano is loud; the demon investigates the Sala while you run to the Kusina |
   | 6 | Bodega | **Tapayan jars:** one of 5 jars holds the candle. Reach in (hold E 2 s); one jar holds rats (jump scare + noise), one is empty, one has the Oratorio key fragment | Reaching in leaves you helpless for 2 s |

5. **Collect the 3 torn pages of the Oración** (randomized locations). Together they give the **order** to light the candles in, as five symbols that match symbols carved on the candle holders in the Oratorio. The order is randomized per run.
6. **Open the Oratorio.** Its key is in two halves (one from the tapayan jars, one held by the faceless santo on the Ang Silid altar). Combine them in the inventory.
7. **Light the five candles in the Oratorio in the correct order.** A wrong order puts out all lit candles in the Oratorio with a gust and roars (the demon comes). Each correct candle increases the ritual phase (Section 5.4).
8. **The final rite** (Section 6.3).
9. **Escape.** The barred front door bursts open. Run through the house down the grand staircase to the Zaguan while the house *shifts*: doors slam, the Gallery lights explode, the staircase partly collapses so you must take the **back stair** loop (scripted, about 60–90 s). Make it a thrilling final run.

### 6.3 The final rite (the climax)

- At the Oratorio altar, hold E to **recite the prayer for 40 s** (the progress shows as lines of prayer text appearing on screen).
- The demon becomes **ENRAGED**. It hunts constantly and can't enter while all 5 candles burn, but it **blows candles out from the doorway** every 8–12 s (visible inhale animation, 1 s telegraph).
- When a candle goes out, the prayer **pauses**. Let go, relight it (costs a match), and resume. If you run out of matches, the spare match pack is somewhere in the Oratorio (a last-second search).
- The flashlight can freeze the demon in the doorway to stop a blow-out (drains the battery 3×).
- If 3 candles are out at once, the demon can enter. Survive by relighting fast.
- At 100 %: the existing banish dissolve plays, then the escape (step 9).

### 6.4 Dawn timer

- The run has a hidden **45-minute** limit (Easy 60, Nightmare 30). The grandfather clock **chimes every 5 minutes**; each chime makes the demon a little faster (+0.05 m/s) and the HUD briefly shows the time.
- At dawn the house "keeps" you: a special bad ending.

### 6.5 Endings

| Ending | How you get it |
|---|---|
| **Banished** (true ending) | Complete the rite and escape. Show stats |
| **Escaped** (secret) | Find the hidden Zaguan door key without doing the ritual and leave. *"You got out. It followed you home."* |
| **Turned** | Caught by the demon → the existing sequence: you become an Esmiringhoy |

## 8. Thrill and atmosphere systems

### 8.1 Fear / composure (0–100)

- Rises: the demon visible or near, standing in darkness without light for long, environmental scares, a CHECK_SPOT on your spot, being chased.
- Falls: standing in light, near a lit candle, hiding calmly while it is far away.
- Effects by level:
  - **40+:** heavier breathing (2 m noise when not holding breath), slight flashlight shake.
  - **60+:** vignette, heartbeat audible, the camera sways slightly.
  - **75+:** **hallucinations:** fake demon silhouettes in dark doorways that vanish when lit, fake footsteps behind you, a fake candle going out in the HUD counter, whispers from inside walls. They're harmless but the player can't be sure.
  - **90+:** brief visual tears / frame stutter, as when the demon stutters.

### 8.2 AudioDirector (dynamic music)

Synthesize original **music stems** with `tools/gen_audio.py` (no samples). All are loopable at the same tempo/length so they crossfade cleanly:
- `mus_drone` (always on, the current house drone),
- `mus_pulse` (demon within 15 m),
- `mus_strings` (HUNT/SEARCH),
- `mus_chase` (CHASE: percussive, frantic).

Rules:
- Crossfade by Director state.
- **Cut everything to near-silence for 1–2 s right before a big scare** (silence is scarier than noise).
- The under-bed check uses its own silence rule (Section 4.5).
- Muffle the audio while holding breath (Section 4.4).

### 8.3 Environmental events (the house is alive)

A pool of events the Director triggers during calm periods (each with a cooldown so they never repeat back to back):
- A door slams somewhere upstairs.
- A piano key plays once by itself in the Sala.
- The family portraits' eyes follow the player (shader UV offset toward the camera).
- Plates fall in the Comedor.
- The rocking chair rocks with no one near.
- A child crying from the Children's Room (stops the moment you enter).
- Knocking from inside the aparador (three knocks; nobody is inside… usually).
- Wet footprints appear along the Gallery, leading to where you are standing.
- The faceless santo is facing a different direction each time you come back.
- Thunder and lightning on the Azotea (lightning lights the whole house for 0.1 s: sometimes the demon is visible somewhere in that flash).
- A whisper: *"Apo… nasaan ka?"* ("Grandchild… where are you?")

Add **2–3 scripted, one-time scares** at fixed story beats (for example the first time you pick up a candle, the first time you enter the Bodega, the first time you open the Oratorio).

### 8.4 Movement and camera additions

- **Crouch** (Ctrl, toggle): 1.3 m/s, quiet, camera at 1.0 m.
- **Lean / peek** (Q / E while not interacting, or rebindable): peek around corners and door frames.
- **Look behind** (hold C while sprinting): see what's chasing you. Essential for chase thrill.
- **Doors:** tap E = open normally (8 m noise); hold E = open slowly (3 m noise). Close doors behind you to slow the demon (it must open or bash them). Doors have real colliders and swing. Locked doors show a lock icon and rattle.
- **Creaky floorboards:** marked by subtly different planks (a visual pattern the player can learn) that creak loudly.

## 11. Technical architecture

### 11.1 Convert the code-built room into editable scenes

The current `room.gd` builds everything in code, so the room can't be edited in the Godot editor. For the house:
- Create **reusable prop scenes** in `scenes/props/` (bed, aparador, door, window_capiz, candle_holder, tapayan, piano, clock, table, paminggalan, tub, carriage, and so on). A prop that can be hidden in contains a `HidingSpot` child.
- Create **one scene per room** in `scenes/rooms/` built from those props, plus `scenes/house.tscn` that assembles the rooms. These must open and be editable in the editor.
- Port the existing Ang Silid faithfully (same positions and tells) into `scenes/rooms/ang_silid.tscn`. It's fine to keep small code helpers for procedural pieces (for example the kulambo or the floor hole).
- Navigation: one `NavigationRegion3D` per floor, baked in the editor (not at runtime). Connect the floors through the stairs' walkable ramps. Use `NavigationLink3D` for the trapdoor (one-way), for ceiling-crawl links and for mirror-walk links.
- Use `OccluderInstance3D` for room walls (occlusion culling) to keep many rooms fast.

### 11.2 Autoloads (singletons)

| Autoload | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `GameState` | Run seed, difficulty, inventory, objective flags, candle/ritual phase, timers, death cause, stats |
| `NoiseBus` | `emit_noise(...)` + signal `noise_emitted` (Section 5.2) |
| `Director` | Menace, pacing, retreats, nudges, event scheduling (Section 5.5) |
| `AudioDirector` | Music stems, silence rules, breath muffle, audio buses (Master / Music / SFX / Voice) |
| `SaveSystem` | Checkpoints + settings (`user://`), using `ConfigFile` or JSON |

### 11.3 Components

- `Interactable` (base class): `prompt_text`, `hold_time`, `can_interact(player) -> bool`, `interact(player)`, signal `interacted`. Doors, pickups, puzzles, candles and hiding spots extend it.
- `HidingSpot`, `Door` (locked id, slow/normal/slam, bashable, broken state), `Pickup`, `PuzzleBase` (and one script per puzzle), `NoteReadable`, `EventTrigger`.
- Player: split `player.gd` into movement, flashlight, interaction, hiding/breath and fear components (child nodes or composed scripts).

### 11.4 Physics layers (extend, don't renumber)

1 world · 2 demon blockers · 3 players · 4 demons · 5 interactables · **6 hiding spots** · **7 doors** · **8 noise occluders** (optional)

### 11.5 Performance budget

- 60 fps at 1080p on a mid-range GPU (GTX 1650 / RX 570 class).
- At most **8 shadow-casting lights** visible at once. Bulbs and candles that are off screen disable shadows. Volumetric fog stays modest.
- Particles: embers and smoke on the demon only. No per-frame allocation in `_process`.

### 11.6 Multiplayer-ready (don't build networking yet)

The original plan is a co-op infection game (caught players become demons). Don't implement networking in this brief. But **route all game-state changes through `GameState` methods** (no scattered direct mutations), so they can become host-authoritative RPCs later.

## 14. Testing (do this throughout)

### 14.1 Commands

```
godot --headless --import                          # import assets, catches parse errors
godot --headless --quit-after 600                  # boot the game for ~10 s
godot --headless -s tests/run_scenarios.gd -- --scenario=<name>
godot --headless -s tests/run_scenarios.gd -- --all
```

On Windows, if `godot` isn't on PATH, ask me for the full path to my Godot `.exe`. For the console variant use `Godot_v4.x-stable_win64_console.exe` so output shows in the terminal.

### 14.2 Harness design

- `tests/run_scenarios.gd` extends `SceneTree`. It loads `res://scenes/main.tscn` (or a test house), then runs a named scenario by **moving the player and controlling inputs directly** (set `global_position`, rotation, `Input.action_press()`, call component methods), stepping time, and asserting outcomes. Print `PASS <name>` / `FAIL <name>: <reason>` and exit with code 1 if anything fails.
- Pattern that worked before: wait for the demon to wake, place the player, watch `demon.state` and `main.game_over`, and print a timeline every 0.5 s while debugging.
- Every new system gets at least one scenario. Fix a failing test before moving on.
- Never ship test-only code in gameplay scripts. Use a `DebugTools` autoload that is only active in debug builds for teleport / give-item / set-phase commands.

### 14.3 Manual playtest checklist (give me one per phase)

Example for Phase 1:
1. Start the game, go to Ang Silid, crawl under the bed **while the demon is far away**. It should search and leave without finding you.
2. Let it see you, then crawl under the bed. It must come straight to the bed and drag you out. The death screen says *"It saw you hide."*
3. Hide under the bed three times in a row. On the third time it should check the bed first.
4. Hide, then don't hold your breath when it's close. It should hear you.
5. Turn the flashlight on inside the wardrobe while it's in the room → caught, with *"It saw your light."*
